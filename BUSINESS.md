# Business Model & Subscription

## Revenue Model

- **Type**: SaaS (Software as a Service) subscription
- **Pricing**: Monthly $9/yearly $90 for families
- **Payment Methods**: Credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Bitcoin, Ethereum

## Subscribed Features

1. Access to app (core kitchen management: pantry, meal planner, shopping lists)
2. Biometric unlock (Face ID / Touch ID integration)
3. Encrypted local backups (SQLCyper encryption)
4. Family sharing (multiple household members, unlimited permissions)
5. Annual security audits and transparency reports

## Free Features Included

- None - All core features require subscription

## Billing Details

- Payment required before app access (no free trial, no freemium tier)
- Subscription can be cancelled at any time (downgrade to lowest tier)
- Annual payment recommended for 20% discount

## Compliance Requirements

- Apple App Store payment processing compliance
- Google Play billing compliance
- GDPR/CCPA data deletion requests handled by subscription cancellation
